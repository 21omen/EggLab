# Style card — Grunge print (1990s / photocopied)

**Use for:** music, skate, streetwear, bars, festivals, anything wanting grit and attitude.
**Say it as:** "beat it up like a 90s gig flyer."

## Palette
- Two inks plus black on off-white: dirty red `#C0392B`, ink blue `#1F3A5F`,
  black `#141414`, paper `#E8E2D5`.
- Colour is uneven — density varies across the sheet.

## Type
- One heavy condensed face slammed to the edges, mixed with a typewriter or mono
  face for details. Mixed case, deliberate collisions.
- Type is allowed to overlap itself and be partly obscured — legibility of the
  *mood* first, the address second (but the key line must still read).

## Image treatment
- Photocopied, thresholded, dithered: photos reduced to 1-bit or coarse halftone,
  often repeated or mirrored.
- Duotone into the palette; edges torn or cut with scissors.

## Ornament vocabulary
Torn paper edges · tape strips · staples · Xerox smear bands · scribbles and
crossed-out words · a rubber-stamp mark · barcode · warning labels · a xeroxed
hand · repeated serial numbers.

## Print artifacts (finish pass)
This style **is** the finish pass: heavy grain, dust and scratches, halftone,
misregistration, high-contrast blowout, visible fold lines. Push all of it.

## Composition
Chaotic but anchored: one dominant block of type, one dominant image, everything
else piled in clusters with a genuinely empty gap between them. Rotation on most
elements — but aligned to a hidden axis so it does not read as accidental.

## Avoid
Clean edges · even ink · soft shadows · glossy gradients · rounded corners ·
perfect alignment of every element · anything that looks professionally laid out.

## World-line seed
"Third-generation photocopy, taped to a venue door, rained on and left to dry."
