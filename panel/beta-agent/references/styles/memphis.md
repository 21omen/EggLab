# Style card — Memphis (1980s)

**Use for:** youth brands, apps, food and drink, festivals, anything wanting playful and loud.
**Say it as:** "treat it like an 80s Memphis poster."

## Palette
- Candy and electric on a light ground: hot pink `#FF5E9C`, turquoise `#2ED9C3`,
  lemon `#FFE44D`, black `#111111`, paper `#F6F3EC`.
- Black is used as a *colour*, not an outline — big black shapes and black type.

## Type
- One heavy geometric or squarish sans, oversized, often tilted or set on a
  baseline that jumps between lines.
- A secondary face in outline (stroke only) for one word, for contrast.

## Image treatment
- Photos are optional; when used, they are hard-cropped, duotoned into the palette,
  or screened with a coarse halftone so they read as a graphic, not a window.

## Ornament vocabulary
Squiggles · confetti dots · terrazzo chips · wavy lines · striped triangles ·
Memphis "boomerang" curves · a black-and-white checker strip · a grid of plus
signs · a tilted rectangle with a thick outline.

## Print artifacts (finish pass)
Grain, one halftone patch, and a slight ink offset on the black. Flat colour,
no gradients anywhere.

## Composition
Deliberately unbalanced: shapes scattered with apparent randomness but clustered
so density varies — a busy corner against an open one. At least one shape runs
off the edge; type overlaps a shape.

## Avoid
Gradients · glows · drop shadows · 3D · realistic rendering · neat equal spacing ·
a pastel-only palette (Memphis has to bite).

## World-line seed
"Screenprinted on gloss card in Milan, 1984, pinned above a foam-block sofa."
