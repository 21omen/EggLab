# Style card — Bauhaus / constructivist geometry

**Use for:** education, design studios, tech launches, exhibitions, anything wanting "principled and modern".
**Say it as:** "build it out of Bauhaus geometry."

## Palette
- Three primaries plus black and white: red `#D8232A`, blue `#1B4B9B`, yellow
  `#F5C400`, black `#000000`, paper `#F2EFE6`.
- Every colour is flat and solid. No gradient, no tint ladder.

## Type
- Geometric sans (Futura spirit) for the headline, set at a steep diagonal or
  stacked in a hard block. Lowercase or all-caps, never small-caps mush.
- Type can be rotated to match a rule or a shape — but always to a deliberate
  angle: 0°, 90°, or one clear diagonal.

## Image treatment
- Photography used as a **photomontage block**: hard-cropped rectangle or circle,
  often high-contrast or duotoned into the palette, sometimes repeated.
- Circle and triangle masks over a photo are period-correct.

## Ornament vocabulary
Circles, triangles, bars · a strong diagonal rule · concentric rings · a numbered
sequence (`1 2 3`) · a thick underline under the headline · quarter-circle forms ·
a grid of small squares.

## Print artifacts (finish pass)
Grain, plus one hard-edged element slightly off-register. Flat inks on matte
paper; no gloss, no bevel, no 3D.

## Composition
Dynamic asymmetry: diagonal energy, big shapes crossing the canvas, type locked
to the geometry rather than floating. Weight sits in one corner and is balanced
by a smaller, denser cluster opposite.

## Avoid
Soft shadows · rounded "friendly" shapes · gradients · photographic realism as
the whole image · centred symmetrical layouts · more than three colours.

## World-line seed
"Lithographed in 1923 on cream stock, three inks, pinned to a studio wall."
