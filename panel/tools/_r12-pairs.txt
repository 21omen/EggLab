@@@REPL
- Generate at the size the asset occupies in the layout (or ~1.5×), not the canvas
  size. A 300 px prop does not need 2048 px — compute the footprint, pass explicit
  width/height, and floor text-bearing assets at 512. Render time scales with area
  (512² ≈ ¼ of 1024²); anything past 2048 is clamped, so upscale in PhotoCraft
  instead of over-rendering.
@@@END
@@@PAIR|R12b enforced envelope + trained buckets
@@@FIND
- **Sizes are multiples of 32.** 1024 is the default on 8 GB; 2048 is the ceiling and
  roughly 4× the cost. Match the ratio to the use case.
@@@REPL
- **Sizes are multiples of 32, 256–2048 enforced.** 1024 is the default on 8 GB;
  2048 is the ceiling and roughly 4× the cost, and the service clamps anything
  larger. Quality peaks in the trained buckets, roughly 1.0–1.8 MP (1328²,
  1664×928, 1472×1104, 1584×1056 and their mirrors). Match the ratio to the use
  case.
@@@END
